The Problem · Deep Space Health Risks SLIDE 02 / 07

The Critical Isolation & Latency Barrier

On missions to the Moon & Mars, signal lag reaches up to 22 minutes each way. Earth flight surgeons cannot intervene in real-time.

Space Radiation

Galactic Cosmic Rays and Solar Particle Events (SPE) damage cellular DNA without atmospheric shielding.

Fluid Shift & SANS

2L of blood shifts headward, raising intracranial pressure and causing Spaceflight-Associated Neuro-ocular Syndrome.

Bone & Muscle Wasting

1.2% bone density loss/month and cardiac remodeling demand rigorous daily countermeasure enforcement.

NASA HRP RIDGE Framework: Radiation · Isolation · Distance · Gravity · Environment
The Solution · AeroHealth OS SLIDE 03 / 07

Autonomous, Offline-First Health Intelligence

A mission control medical cockpit built for astronauts, not just clinicians. Works seamlessly with zero ground connection.

Interactive Anatomical Assessment

Clickable zero-G astronaut figure mapping symptoms directly to organ systems with plain-language guidance.

Edge AI Clinical Decision Support

Real-time triage (Green/Yellow/Red) recommending step-by-step actions based on NASA HRP medical protocols.

Gamified Daily Routine Hub

Turns arduous ARED workouts, fluid loading, and PVT alertness tests into engaging quests with streaks and XP.

Multi-Environment Adaptability

Instant toggle between ISS (LEO), Lunar Gateway (Artemis), and Mars Transit with dynamic latency and radiation modeling.

Architecture: Zero-dependency, offline-ready, responsive HTML5/Canvas/Chart.js engine.
NASA Core Requirement · Interactive Diagnostics SLIDE 04 / 07

Translating Telemetry into Intuitive Diagnostics

Astronauts can click any body region to understand Why the exam is needed, How hazards affect it, and What to do.

Eye Node (SANS)

Tracks intracranial pressure & optic disc edema. Triggers tonometry and LBNP countermeasure guidance.

Cardiac Node

Continuous Lead II ECG streaming, detecting ventricular ectopy and fluid shift induced hypovolemia.

Musculoskeletal Node

Monitors ARED resistance volume and spinal elongation to prevent disc herniation during EVAs.

Playable NASA PVT-B Reaction Test: Directly interactive in the web app to test cognitive lapses!
